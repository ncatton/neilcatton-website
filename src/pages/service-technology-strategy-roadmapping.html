Before you book

How long does the review take?

Typically four to six weeks, run as a fixed-scope engagement rather than an open-ended retainer.

What do we actually receive at the end?

A written strategy the executive team can present to the board in its own words, a prioritised roadmap with dependencies made explicit, and business cases stress-tested for the trade-offs they actually contain.

How is this different from a strategy a vendor writes for us?

A vendor-written strategy usually has something to sell behind it. This is independent: no platform, licence or delivery contract depends on the recommendation, so the sequencing reflects what the organisation actually needs rather than what closes a deal.

How is it commercially structured?

A fixed fee, agreed before the work starts, not a day rate that runs open-ended.

What happens after the roadmap is delivered?

It’s built to be used without further involvement: a common frame the organisation can keep using once the engagement ends.
