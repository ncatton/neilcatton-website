Before you book

What’s the time commitment?

Typically one to two days a month, plus committee work: audit and risk, or technology scrutiny on M&A activity.

What does the board actually gain?

Technology stops arriving at the board as a finished recommendation and starts arriving as a decision the board can genuinely interrogate, with risks named before they’re authorised, not after they’ve matured.

Is this a technical role or a governance one?

Governance, informed by technical depth. The value is independent scrutiny at board level, not a technologist sitting in on meetings.

What kind of organisations is this suited to?

Boards where technology has become material to the risk register, through AI adoption, an acquisition, a transformation programme or regulatory change, but no one at the table can interrogate it independently.

How is it commercially structured?

An annual fee, agreed as part of the appointment.
