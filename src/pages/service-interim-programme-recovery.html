Before you book

How quickly can this start?

This exists for situations the organisation cannot afford to leave open. The intent is to start this month, not after a search process concludes.

How long does an interim engagement typically run?

Typically three to nine months, structured around stabilising the position, delivering and handing over in good order.

What do we get in the first month?

An honest assessment of the actual position, including the uncomfortable parts, rather than a diplomatic summary that avoids naming what’s wrong.

Does this include help hiring a permanent replacement?

Yes, if wanted. The handover at the end of the engagement can include running that hiring process.

How is it commercially structured?

A day rate or a monthly retainer, depending on the shape of the engagement, for a full-time or near full-time commitment.
