Before you book

Is this for pre-close diligence or after a deal has completed?

Either. The engagement is scoped to fit inside a live deal window pre-close, or to size the gap between the data room and reality after completion.

How long does it take?

Typically two to four weeks, agreed to fit the deal timetable rather than the other way round.

What does the deal team actually get?

A written assessment framed commercially, what’s there, what it would cost to fix or scale and what that means for the deal, plus a risk register with sizes attached.

Does the review cover the team, not just the technology?

Yes, including where the knowledge actually sits and what leaves if particular people do, which a purely technical audit usually misses.

How is it commercially structured?

A fixed fee, agreed before the review starts and set against the deal window.
