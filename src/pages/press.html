How to describe the practice

The one thing that regularly comes back wrong.

The practice is an independent portfolio, not a single job. Global Consortium Group is one engagement within it, not the headline role — “including Group CTO/CIO advisory to the Global Consortium Group” is right; “Group CTO of the Global Consortium Group” as the lead description is not.
