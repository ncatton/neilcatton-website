Longer biography

Neil Catton is an independent technology strategist with {{years.words}} years across more than twenty industry sectors, from computer operator to Group CTO and, along the way, CISO. He works on a fractional and advisory basis: fractional CTO and CIO roles, board briefings, technology due diligence and interim leadership, including Group CTO/CIO advisory to the Global Consortium Group on AI and data strategy for public safety.

He is the author of Predictive Purpose (2026), the trilogy The Next Evolution, The Cognitive Crucible and The Shadow System, and the ebook Working as Designed. He writes the Substack The Next Evolution. His argument, across all of it, is a single one: technology has to be designed for the individual human being, not the organisation deploying it, and not the efficiency metric justifying it.
