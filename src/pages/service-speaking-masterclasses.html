Before you book

What formats are available?

Keynote, masterclass or offsite session, running from thirty minutes to half a day depending on the format.

Who is this suited to?

Conferences, boards and leadership teams: anywhere a technology session needs to change the questions people ask, not just show them slides.

Is this a vendor pitch in keynote form?

No. There’s no platform or product behind the talk, which is precisely what most conference and board audiences are tired of being sold.

What does preparation involve?

A planning call beforehand to shape the session around the actual audience, and materials provided afterwards where they’re useful rather than as standard.

How is it commercially structured?

A fixed fee plus travel, agreed before the booking is confirmed.
