Before you book

How much time does a fractional CTO/CIO engagement take?

Typically one to three days a week, agreed at the outset and reviewed as the organisation’s needs change.

Is this remote or on-site?

Both: a mix of remote and on-site work, based in the UK.

How long does the engagement usually run?

Typically three to twelve months, reviewed quarterly rather than fixed in advance.

What happens when we’re ready to hire a permanent CTO or CIO?

The engagement includes running that hiring process when the time comes, handled by someone who has held the role rather than a recruiter working from a job description alone.

How is it commercially structured?

A monthly retainer, set for the agreed number of days.
