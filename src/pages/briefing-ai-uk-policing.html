What’s in the briefing

The case that carries it

In November 2025 West Midlands Police gave a Safety Advisory Group a written risk report containing a claimed previous fixture between Maccabi Tel Aviv and West Ham that had never happened. The claim came from an unauthorised Microsoft Copilot query, and it was not initially disclosed.

Away fans were banned from Villa Park, the decision became a national political argument, the Chief Constable retired, and the IOPC opened an independent investigation that includes a voluntary conduct referral about the force’s use of AI.

The Home Affairs Committee established all of this on the record in February 2026. It made no recommendation about police use of AI at all.

Questions people ask before reading

Is this vendor material?

No. Every vendor claim encountered in the research, including facial recognition accuracy percentages, time-saving claims for AI report-writing tools and detection rates for digital triage, was excluded, because none of it traces to an operator speaking on its own record.

Is it free to download?

Yes. It downloads directly, with no form and no email address required.

Is this an argument against police use of AI?

No. It is an accountability argument, not a privacy or technology one, and it is made entirely from policing’s own institutions. The question it ends on is a practical one a chief officer has to answer personally: when a tool bought nationally and assured nationally produces a wrong fact that reaches a decision, whose name is on it?

How current is the research?

Verified against primary sources as at 14 September 2026, including HMICFRS State of Policing 2025–26 published on 8 September 2026, the IOPC’s deaths during or following police contact figures for 2025/26, the ICO’s facial recognition audit findings of 18 August 2026, and the PoliceAI announcement of 10 June 2026.

Does it name forces?

Where a regulator, an inspectorate, a select committee or a court has made a finding on the public record. The West Midlands Police case is covered in detail because the Home Affairs Committee, the IOPC and HMICFRS have each put it on the record. Individual officers are not named.

What if I want to take this further?
