Before you book

How long does the initial review take?

Typically six to ten weeks, whether it runs as a fixed-scope review or the start of an ongoing advisory relationship.

What does “current-state map” actually mean in practice?

A view of what the organisation’s systems actually depend on, tested against the systems themselves rather than assumed from an old diagram, so decisions are made on what’s true, not what’s documented.

What do we receive?

A current-state view, a target architecture sized to what the organisation actually needs rather than an imported reference model, and a sequenced plan for closing the gap with the trade-offs named.

Is this a one-off review or an ongoing relationship?

Either. Some organisations need the initial review only; others keep the engagement running as ongoing advisory once the initial gap is mapped.

How is it commercially structured?

A fixed fee for the initial review, or a day rate if it continues as ongoing advisory.
