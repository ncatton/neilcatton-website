Before you book

How long does the review take?

Four to six weeks, run as a fixed-scope engagement.

What’s the difference between this and an AI strategy document?

A strategy document sets ambition. This states the organisation’s actual position: what data it holds, who governs it, which decisions AI is already shaping. It then sequences the decisions that follow, so it can be acted on the week it arrives rather than filed.

What do we receive?

A written assessment in language an executive team and board can both read, a sequenced set of decisions, what to do first and what to leave until later, and a plain statement of the real risk exposure.

Is this only for organisations already using AI?

No. It’s equally useful before committing to a pilot, when ambition is ahead of the organisation’s actual knowledge of itself.

How is it commercially structured?

A fixed fee, agreed before the review starts.
