Three decades of model risk governance

1996Basel’s Market Risk Amendment sets the foundations: board accountability, backtesting, stress testing.2011SR 11-7 becomes the first comprehensive model risk framework.2026Its revision, SR 26-2, explicitly excludes generative and agentic AI from scope.

The human moment

Every framework lands, in the end, on one signature.

  1. We know what models we are running
  2. They behave as validated
  3. Validation is current in substance, not only in date
  4. No model decides without a human review point
  5. The expertise to challenge remains in-house

That is what an attestation of effective model risk management implicitly claims. Current deployment conditions give reasonable cause to doubt all five.

Why it matters now
