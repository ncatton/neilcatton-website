The same failures, wherever you look

Three patterns run through finance. None is unique to it.

Correct at every step, wrong at the end

1 of its 6 essays is in finance.

No individual part of the system fails. Every department, every agent, every board process does exactly what it was designed to do, and the outcome is still wrong, with nobody positioned to own the gap.

Built to be defensible, not to help

3 of its 8 essays are in finance.

Institutional response systems for fraud, breach and cybersecurity are built to produce a record that the institution acted, not to close the exposure or protect the person living inside it.

The permanent record you didn’t choose

1 of its 4 essays is in finance.

Data about a person is captured once and becomes a durable, portable record used to make decisions about them, with no mechanism for the person to see it, correct it, or reclaim it.
