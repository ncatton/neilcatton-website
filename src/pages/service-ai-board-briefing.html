Before you book

How long is the session?

Half a day, prepared in advance around the board’s actual agenda rather than a generic AI deck.

In the boardroom or remote, whichever suits the board, based in the UK.

A written summary of what was discussed, decided and left open, circulated within the week, plus a set of questions the board can put to the executive team at every future AI decision.

No. There is no platform or licence behind the recommendation. The point of the session is a plain assessment of where the organisation’s real exposure sits, not a pitch for a particular tool.
