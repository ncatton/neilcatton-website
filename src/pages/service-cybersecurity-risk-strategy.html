Before you book

How long does the assessment take?

Typically four to six weeks, whether run as a fixed-scope review or ongoing board advisory.

How is this different from a standard security audit?

A standard audit usually checks controls against a framework. This states the actual exposure in business terms, what’s exposed, what it would cost and what’s being accepted rather than managed, informed by having held a CISO role, not just advised on one.

What do we receive?

A written risk posture assessment and a prioritised set of recommendations sized to actual impact rather than vendor enthusiasm, in board-ready form.

Is this pitching a particular security product?

No. There’s no tool or platform behind the recommendations, which is precisely the gap this fills for boards used to being sold a security stack alongside the advice.

How is it commercially structured?

A fixed fee, agreed before the review starts.
