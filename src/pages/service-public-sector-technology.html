Before you book

What sectors does this cover?

Central government, health, justice, education and home affairs. Engagements are shaped to fit each sector’s own governance and procurement realities.

What shape can the engagement take?

Fractional, interim or advisory, depending on what the organisation needs. The commercial basis follows from that shape.

Is this remote or on-site?

Both: a mix of remote and on-site work, based in the UK.

What’s different about public sector technology leadership specifically?

Timelines and delivery partner promises are often built on a private-sector decision cycle that doesn’t match how public sector governance actually moves. This starts from having sat inside that governance structure before, not learning it on the client’s time.

Does this include holding delivery partners to account?

Yes. That’s a specific, recurring part of the work, done by someone who has been on the delivery-partner side of that relationship as well as the client side.
