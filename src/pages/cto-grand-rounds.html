Sessions

Cases reviewed

Each session brings two or three independent fractional CTOs together to question a live technical or organisational problem — the panel is free to disagree with each other, and the founder leaves with a written diagnosis rather than a single unchallenged opinion. This page collects the sessions Neil has taken part in.

  • LinkEDtohome — Scaling, safeguarding and AI governance
    Second session · 26 August 2026 · Founder: Susie Williams

    Susie Williams, CEO and co-founder of LinkEDtohome — which supports schools, parents and pupils with consistent safeguarding and wellbeing — brought the company’s next stage of growth to the panel: how to think about scaling the team and the technology together, and the questions of AI liability, training and governance that come with it. Neil Catton and Jerome MacGillivray reviewed the case together, curated by Jane Vyshnova, Founder of CTO Grand Rounds. Both panellists already knew the realities of working in education, safeguarding included, so the session started at the problem rather than at the briefing.

    “The most useful part for me was how well matched Neil and Jerome were to our business.” Susie Williams, CEO & Co-Founder, LinkEDtohome

Why this fits.

Fractional and interim technology leadership depends on judgement formed outside any single organisation. Grand Rounds sessions are one place that judgement gets tested in the open — a live case, a panel that disagrees where it disagrees, and a founder who leaves with something usable rather than a verdict agreed before the call started.

Neil takes part as one of the Attending Panel's fractional CTOs, alongside a rotating group of senior technology leaders drawn from across the network. Sessions vary case to case — this page collects the ones he has been part of, as they happen.
