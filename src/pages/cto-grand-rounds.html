How it runs

A panel that disagrees where it disagrees.

  1. The caseA live technical or organisational problem, brought by the founder.
  2. The sessionIndependent CTOs question it together, live.
  3. The disagreementIn the open, not agreed before the call started.
  4. The follow-upA written diagnosis within 48 hours.

What the founder leaves with

  • A written diagnosis
  • Something usable, rather than a single unchallenged opinion
