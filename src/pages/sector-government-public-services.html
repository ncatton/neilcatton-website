The same failures, wherever you look

Four patterns run through government and public services. None is unique to it.

Correct at every step, wrong at the end

2 of its 6 essays are in government and public services.

No individual part of the system fails. Every department, every agent, every board process does exactly what it was designed to do, and the outcome is still wrong, with nobody positioned to own the gap.

The dashboard stays green

1 of its 7 essays is in government and public services.

Measurement and consultation substitute for genuine understanding. The organisation collects a number or a response, treats the collecting as the work, and never asks whether it changed anything.

Designed for the person who isn’t there

2 of its 10 essays are in government and public services.

Systems are built around the capable, average, organised user, and fail exactly at the point where the person in front of them is least able to advocate for themselves.

The permanent record you didn’t choose

1 of its 4 essays is in government and public services.

Data about a person is captured once and becomes a durable, portable record used to make decisions about them, with no mechanism for the person to see it, correct it, or reclaim it.
